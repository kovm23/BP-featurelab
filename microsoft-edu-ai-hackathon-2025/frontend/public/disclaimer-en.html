Data Protection, Fair Use & Usage Agreement

For the experimental web application at llmfeatures.vse.cz.

1. Mandatory Data Anonymization Request

To protect privacy and comply with data protection regulations, including GDPR, remove all personally identifiable information (PII), confidential data, and sensitive data from source files before uploading them for analysis.

This open-source and experimental tool analyzes and extracts features from the substance of research and multimedia data. It is not designed to process personal details.

Please review datasets and strip out:

  • Author or subject names, institutional affiliations, email addresses, and contact details.
  • Acknowledgments, funding statements, metadata, or document properties that identify individuals.
  • Proprietary, commercial, confidential, or sensitive corporate data.
  • Any unstructured element that could identify you, co-authors, participants, or research subjects.

2. Fair Use & Acceptable Use Policy

This tool is provided for research, academic, and analytical purposes. To protect shared access and system security, users must follow these conditions:

  • No prompt injection or manipulation. Do not submit hidden prompts, jailbreaks, or adversarial instructions intended to alter workflows, system prompts, or safety controls.
  • No sideloading or malicious payloads. Do not inject scripts, executable content, unauthorized data, arbitrary code, or bypass intended graphical or API interfaces.
  • Intended use only. Use the service only for discovering and generating human-readable features from valid multimodal datasets, including text, images, tables, and video.
  • Resource protection. Do not intentionally overload the service with excessive, automated, or concurrent requests, including denial-of-service attacks or aggressive scraping.
